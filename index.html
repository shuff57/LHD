Answer key

One teacher, one left hand, and a habit of fixing the thing that slowed class down.

  1. Who writes this? Steven Huff: math educator, CS teacher and curriculum developer, ten-plus years from elementary through college. The full CV lives at steven.lefthanddev.com.
  2. Why "Left Hand"? Lefties drag their hand through wet ink, so every line gets checked twice. The smudge is a feature.
  3. Why the capital SH? They're the initials. rāSHio, bookSHelf, verSHion: the tools sign their own names.
  4. What's the rule? A student should open the page, get the answer, and get back to the homework. Student data stays on the student's machine wherever possible.
