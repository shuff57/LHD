  1. Why "Left Hand"?

    Because I’m left-handed. Lefties drag their hand through wet ink, so every line gets checked twice. The smudge is a feature.

  2. Why the capital SH?

    They're the initials. rāSHio, bookSHelf, verSHion: the tools sign their own names.

  3. What's the rule?

    A student should open the page, get the answer, and get back to the homework. Student data stays on the student's machine wherever possible.
